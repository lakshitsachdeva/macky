Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Recurdo ("we", "us", "our") is a macOS application that indexes your local files using an on-device AI model. This policy explains what we collect and what we don't.

1. What we collect

We collect only your email address when you join the waitlist. Nothing else.

2. What we never collect

Recurdo does not collect, transmit, or store any of your files, file names, file contents, screenshots, email attachments, or any other data processed by the local AI model. All indexing and search runs entirely on your device.

3. How we use your email

Your email is used solely to notify you when the Recurdo macOS app is ready. We will not send marketing emails, share your address with third parties, or add you to any other lists without explicit consent.

4. Data storage

Waitlist emails are stored with a third-party email provider (currently Resend). You may request deletion at any time by emailing lakshits.official@gmail.com.

5. Analytics

The waitlist page may use privacy-respecting analytics (no cookies, no fingerprinting) to count page visits only.

6. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will notify waitlist members by email before the change takes effect.
